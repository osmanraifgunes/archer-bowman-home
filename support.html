Frequently asked questions

How do I shoot?

On a phone, put your finger down anywhere on the right side of the screen and swipe toward the enemies: the direction sets the angle and the length of the swipe sets the power. Let go to fire. The dots in front of your hero show where the arrow will fly. On the left side, a joystick moves your hero.

How do I win a level?

Your soldiers win when one of them reaches the enemy flag; you lose when an enemy reaches yours. Shoot the enemies before they can push through – a level can’t be won without your arrows.

How do I earn more coins?

Every enemy you shoot down gives coins, and headshots pay more. Daily rewards, the free chest (every 8 hours) and daily quests give extra coins. After a win you can watch an optional ad to double the coins. Coins are kept even if you lose a level.

How do stars and medals work?

3 stars: win with your hero alive and at least 60% health · 2 stars: win with your hero alive · 1 star: win after your hero fell. Every 3-star level gives a medal, and beating a King or a special boss gives 3. Medals unlock fire and ice arrows and new heroes.

I can’t beat a level. Any tips?

Aim for the head – headshots deal much more damage. Shoot enemy archers and healers first. Upgrade in the shop (Sharp Arrows and Quick Draw help the most early on), and replay earlier levels for coins and stars.

I lost my progress / I changed my phone.

Your progress is saved only on your device, so it is removed when the Game is uninstalled or the app data is cleared, and it does not move to a new phone. We are sorry – we can’t restore it from our side.

How do I redeem a creator code?

Open Settings > Creator code on the main menu and type the code. Each player can redeem one code, once.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings and tap the language row. The Game supports English, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, Français, العربية, বাংলা, Português, Русский, اردو, Deutsch and Türkçe.

How do I turn off reminders or personalised ads?

Reminders can be turned off in your phone’s notification settings for Archer Bowman. For ads and tracking choices, see the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the Game to delete your saved progress. For data processed by our advertising and attribution partners, see the Privacy Policy or write to us.
