Privacy Policy

Archer Bowman: Bow & Arrow

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Archer Bowman: Bow & Arrow (“the Game”), published by Mobinavel (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play it on Android or iOS.

In short: the Game has no accounts and we do not ask for your name, e-mail or any other personal details. Your progress is stored only on your device. To show ads and to measure where installs come from, the Game uses two third-party services – Google AdMob and AppsFlyer – which may process device identifiers as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades, medals, daily rewards and quests, language and sound settings, a redeemed creator code) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game, or using Settings > Reset progress, deletes it.

2. Advertising – Google AdMob

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for example “Watch ad x2 coins” or a revive) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA – on iOS only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address, device model, operating system, language and app information,
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the Game shows Google’s consent form (UMP) before ads are personalised. To change your choice later, clear the Game’s data (or reinstall it) and the form will be shown again, or contact us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Install attribution – AppsFlyer

We use AppsFlyer to understand which advertising campaigns, creators or links led to an install. AppsFlyer may process:

  • advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID; Apple IDFA only if you allow tracking; IDFV), the Google Play Install Referrer,
  • IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, install and open times,
  • a few game events: tutorial completed, certain level milestones reached, a creator code redeemed, and ad revenue from AdMob.

See the AppsFlyer Privacy Policy. You can opt out of AppsFlyer measurement on their opt-out page.

4. Your choices about tracking

  • iPhone / iPad: the Game asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your IDFA is not shared. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (daily reward, free chest, streak and “your army is waiting” reminders). They are created on the device itself – no push server and no personal data are involved. You can turn them off in your phone’s settings.

6. In-app purchases

The Game currently has no in-app purchases. Coins and upgrades are earned only by playing.

8. Children

Archer Bowman is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game and data was processed by our partners, please contact us and we will help to have it deleted.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data processed by Google and AppsFlyer is kept according to their own policies. To ask for deletion of attribution data linked to your device, contact us with your device’s advertising ID, or use the opt-out options above.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Security

Our partners transfer data over encrypted connections. As we do not run our own servers for the Game, we do not store your data ourselves.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
