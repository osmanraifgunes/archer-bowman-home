Every arrow counts

You are the champion archer of a brave little army. Your soldiers march toward the enemy flag, the enemy marches toward yours – and only your bow can tip the battle.

🏹

Skill-based archery

Real arcing arrows, wind, headshots and piercing shots. Swipe to aim – the longer the swipe, the stronger the shot.

🗺️

Hundreds of levels

Meadows, deserts, snowfields, stormy coasts, harbours, volcanoes and crystal caves – and new worlds keep coming.

👑

Kings & bosses

Shield bearers, spearmen, diving eagles, hidden ambushers, wolf packs, battering rams and mighty kings.

🛡️

Upgrade your hero

Sharper arrows, multishot, stronger armour – earn a golden bow and a royal cape as your hero grows.

🔥

Fire & ice arrows

Earn medals to unlock special arrows and a second hero: the Crossbowman, whose bolts pierce shields.

🎁

Daily rewards

A 7-day reward streak, a free chest every 8 hours and three new daily quests.

How to win

Easy to learn, satisfying to master.

Swipe & shoot

Swipe toward the enemies to aim and set the power, let go to fire.

Aim for the head

Body hits slow enemies down – headshots hit harder and pay more coins.

Reach the flag

Your army wins when a soldier reaches the enemy flag. Don't let them reach yours!

Come back stronger

Spend your coins in the shop on permanent upgrades. Coins are kept even when you lose.
